# Methodology surface brief

Scope: Methodology page inside the established shell. Mode: Read. Audience: researchers and
journalists checking how the archive was made before citing it. Job: find the collection rules,
schemas, semantic methods and limits, and see data notices that change how periods compare.
Decisions: data notices lead the article; two statements were updated to stay true (Cloudflare
dispatch, visible-headline extraction) and a headline-text notice was added.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A news-article reading page for the archive's methods, with the notices that change
interpretation placed first; it refuses the documentation-site default of a hero and a grey sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: White canvas, #141414 ink, #DDDDDD rules, ink section rules, surface-grey callouts under a
2px ink rule, red links and current-section marker. Inter body at 1.0625rem/1.65 in a 68ch column;
monospace only for field names and code.

STORY: The reader learns what is unreliable first, then how collection, storage, migration,
validation, semantics and the interface work, and where the limits are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title, standfirst, version meta line; left contents list with the current section
in red; article column opening on Data notices with the outage, reduced-rate and headline-text
callouts.

FORM: Extension of the established Overview world, Read mode. seed: none (roll skipped: direction pinned by the user)
Pin, in the user's words: "I'm looking at a visual overhaul in line with the design pattern that
exists on the standard https://www.bbc.co.uk/news website" and "I would like to use the BBC red,
and that's that".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
