# Trends surface brief

Scope: /trends/ inside the established shell. Mode: Operate. Audience: researchers reading
aggregate patterns. Job: read the latest findings, inspect one signal over time, and browse
recurring stories with their article timelines. Data: semantic-findings, semantic-trends,
recurring-events via the existing signals worker, plus daily.json for collected days.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Findings, a daily series and recurring stories set as ruled news sections; it refuses
the dashboard default of metric cards and dark chart panels.

OWN-WORLD: White canvas, ink type and line chart, hairline column rules, surface-grey shading for
days without collection, ink share bars, red ask links, story rows beside a white record.

STORY: The visitor reads what is rising and where the lists differ, checks whether the sample is
reliable, inspects a signal's daily series, then opens a recurring story's timeline.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and one line; status bar; Latest findings with window and sample warning;
four ruled columns (rising themes, list differences, story forms, returning stories).

FORM: Extension of the established Overview world. seed: none (roll skipped: direction pinned by the user)
Pin, in the user's words: "I'm looking at a visual overhaul in line with the design pattern that
exists on the standard https://www.bbc.co.uk/news website" and "I would like to use the BBC red,
and that's that". The user chose to split Ask & analyse into two pages.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
